# Energy Operations & Logistics Optimization Dashboard

##  Project Status

**Project in Progress**

This project is part of my **Energy, Supply Chain & Operations Analytics Portfolio**.

The objective is to explore how production, transportation, inventory, and scheduling data can be combined to identify operational bottlenecks and inefficiencies.

**Important:** This is an independent portfolio project and does not use confidential Cenovus operational data.

---

##  Problem Statement

Production does not operate in isolation.

Transportation capacity, inventory availability, scheduling constraints, and logistics performance can influence how efficiently products move through an operation.

The analytical question is:

**Can operational and logistics data be used to identify bottlenecks between production, transportation, inventory, and scheduling?**

---

##  Proposed Solution

I plan to build an:

### **Energy Operations & Logistics Optimization Dashboard**

The dashboard will provide visibility into:

* Production volumes
* Transportation movements
* Inventory levels
* Scheduling performance
* Capacity utilization
* Operational bottlenecks

---

##  Key Analytical Questions

1. How does production vary over time?
2. How efficiently is production transported?
3. Where do transportation bottlenecks occur?
4. Are inventory levels aligned with production and transportation?
5. Which routes or assets experience capacity constraints?
6. Where do scheduling delays occur?
7. Are there recurring operational bottlenecks?
8. Which KPIs provide the clearest view of operational performance?
